Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy explains what Sooz Beverage, Inc. ("Sooz", "we") collects when you use drinksooz.com, join our waitlist or newsletter, buy from us, or interact with our social media accounts, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Things you give us: your email address when you join the waitlist or newsletter, your name, email and message when you contact us, and the details you enter at checkout.
  • Referrals: if you arrive through a creator or referral link, we record which link sent you so we can credit the right person.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To send you the updates, newsletter issues and launch news you signed up for.
  • To answer your messages and fulfil your orders.
  • To run our referral and creator programs.
  • To understand how the site is used and make it better.

We do not sell your personal information.

Social media and our posting tools

We publish our own videos to our own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts, sometimes using those platforms' official APIs (including the TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube API Services). These tools only access accounts that Sooz owns and only to upload and publish our content. We do not use them to collect data about other users. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access our tools have been given can be revoked at any time from Google security settings or the TikTok app.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the site for us: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), Resend (email), Stripe (payments) and Google (analytics). They may process your data only to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are on our list or a customer, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing risus@drinksooz.com.
  • You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
